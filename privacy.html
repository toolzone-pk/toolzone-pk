Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

🔒 Short version: We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. Your files stay on your device.

// 1. Information We Collect

ToolZone does not require any account creation or login. We do not collect any personally identifiable information. Any files you upload to our tools (images, PDFs, etc.) are processed entirely in your browser and are never sent to our servers.

// 2. Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic, anonymous analytics to understand how many visitors use the site. This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

// 3. Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts to load typography. Google may collect basic request data (such as your IP address) as part of serving these fonts. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

// 4. Your Data Security

Since all tool processing happens locally in your browser, your files and sensitive information never leave your device. We have no access to any content you process using ToolZone tools.

// 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of ToolZone after changes means you accept the updated policy.

// 6. Contact
